# teamdiff

A terminal companion for VALORANT on Windows. teamdiff reads the Riot Client running on your PC and
shows your lobby as the game moves through menus, agent select, the match and the post-game screen:
who is in it, their ranks and recent form from tracker.gg, and how the match went.

teamdiff is in early development and doesn't do much yet. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains
how to build it.
